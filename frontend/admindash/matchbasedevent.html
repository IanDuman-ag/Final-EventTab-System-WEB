Automatic System Workflow

  • Tournament brackets are automatically updated after the Faculty confirms the match result.
  • Winners automatically advance to the next round; losers are eliminated per the selected format.
  • The next match schedule is automatically generated and updated.
  • Final rankings, Champion, and Runner-Up are automatically computed when the tournament ends.
  • Championship points are automatically applied after official results are published.
  • The Overall Leaderboard and department standings are automatically updated.
  • Official results become visible to viewers immediately after publication and are locked.
